About Our Foundation

Rooted in Care, Driven by Community

How We Started

In the spring of 2018, our founder and a handful of local residents gathered to clean an abandoned, deforested creek bed in our hometown. Shocked by the rapid decline of urban greenery, we planted 200 native saplings in a single weekend. What started as a modest neighborhood effort quickly sparked a movement. Today, Green Earth Foundation connects thousands of environmental advocates across continents to protect wild biodiversity and build climate resilience.

Our Mission

To rehabilitate degraded natural landscapes, foster ecological balance, and equip people everywhere with the tools to champion conservation in their backyards.

Our Vision

A flourishing world where living canopies thrive, plastic-free waters flow naturally, and communities act in harmonious stewardship with mother nature.

What We Do

Our Environmental Services

From mountain ridges to sandy shorelines and inner-city classrooms, we implement comprehensive green solutions designed for lasting ecological impact.

Reforestation

Tree Planting & Forestry

We plant indigenous, drought-resilient tree species in deforested areas, watershed borders, and urban heat islands. Every grove is scientifically cataloged and tended until maturity.

  • 92%+ Sapling 3-Year Survival Rate
  • Native Pollinator & Wildlife Habitats
  • Urban Green Canopy Expansion
Waste Reduction

Clean-Up Drives

We organize grassroots clean-up expeditions across coastlines, river basins, and public parks. Collected plastic, toxic micro-waste, and scrap materials are responsibly sorted for recycling.

  • Zero-Waste Sorting & Circular Recycling
  • River Mouth & Coastal Debris Barriers
  • Safety Gear & Eco Burlap Kits Provided
Eco Education

Awareness Workshops

Education breeds conservation. We conduct interactive youth masterclasses, school assemblies, and corporate green seminars teaching composting, zero-waste living, and native biodiversity preservation.

  • K-12 Curriculum & Student Seedling Labs
  • Home Composting & Rainwater Harvesting
  • Corporate Sustainability Certifications
