Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

At Green Earth Foundation ("we", "our", or "us"), we are deeply committed to protecting the personal privacy and data of our donors, volunteers, event participants, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is safeguarded, and your choices regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you intentionally provide to us. This may include:

  • Donor Information: Name, email address, billing address, phone number, and transaction records when making a contribution. Payment card details are tokenized and processed securely through PCI-DSS certified gateways; we never store raw credit/debit card numbers.
  • Volunteer Details: Name, contact details, city/region, area of conservation interest, and availability for tree planting or clean-up events.
  • Inquiries & Contact: Information submitted via our contact forms or email correspondence.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Email address for our monthly conservation updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly utilized to advance our environmental conservation initiatives:

  • To process and issue official tax-deductible donation receipts.
  • To coordinate logistics for tree planting drives, shoreline cleanups, and eco-workshops.
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests.
  • To communicate project milestones, audited impact reports, and upcoming events.

3. Zero Sale or Commercial Sharing of Data

Green Earth Foundation operates strictly as a non-profit organization. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third-party marketers or commercial entities under any circumstances.

4. Data Security

We implement 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all website communications, firewall protections, and restricted administrative access protocols to ensure your data remains confidential and secure from unauthorized access.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You maintain full control over your personal information at all times:

  • You may unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom of any newsletter.
  • You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you or ask us to update or delete your volunteer profile by contacting our Data Protection Officer.

6. Contact Our Privacy Team

If you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us:
