Terms of Service

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to the Green Earth Foundation website and community platform. By accessing our website, making donations, or enrolling as an environmental volunteer, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following Terms of Service.

1. Our Non-Profit Purpose

Green Earth Foundation is an environmental non-profit organization dedicated to native reforestation, marine and coastline cleanup drives, and community ecological education. All resources, donations, and volunteer efforts are dedicated exclusively to our environmental conservation missions.

2. Volunteer Participation Guidelines

Participation in our community events is voluntary. By registering for tree planting or cleanup drives:

  • You agree to follow safety instructions provided by designated event coordinators and wear recommended protective equipment (gloves, sturdy shoes).
  • Volunteers under 18 years of age must have parent or guardian consent before participating in outdoor field activities.
  • Green Earth Foundation strives to provide safe, welcoming environments; participants acknowledge inherent outdoor activities involve natural elements and agree to exercise personal care.

3. Donations & Financial Transparency

All charitable contributions made through this website are applied directly towards our environmental conservation projects, sapling procurement, nursery maintenance, and cleanup supplies. Official tax-deductible receipts are issued immediately via email upon transaction confirmation.

  • Donations may be configured as one-time contributions or monthly recurring donations.
  • Recurring monthly gifts may be altered or cancelled at any time by contacting our support team or through your confirmation link.

4. Intellectual Property & Photography

All text, photographs, graphic elements, and brand logos on this website are the property of Green Earth Foundation or used with permission. Photography captured during public volunteer events may be featured in our impact reports and gallery to showcase community achievements.

5. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms from time to time to reflect organizational or regulatory requirements. Continued engagement with our services following updates constitutes acceptance of the modified terms.
